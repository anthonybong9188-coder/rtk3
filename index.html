    Generals

    Each general charges into battle with his troops. Generals with War above 80 add 1% damage per point, so Guan Yu (97) hits 17% harder.

    Duels

    Officers with War 70+ can challenge an adjacent officer once. Five rounds of Strike, Guard or Feint. Read the enemy's tell; it is right about two times in three. The loser's unit loses 30% of its troops and misses its next turn.

    Victory

    Break the enemy commander's force (gold border) or every enemy unit. The attacker has 15 turns; after that the defender wins. You lose if your own commander's force breaks. Tap an enemy to attack it; your unit moves into range on its own.

    Specials (Shu only)

    Charge (cavalry): ×1.5 damage, weak counter, rides through to the square behind. Brace (spearmen): until Wei's turn ends, intercepts cavalry attacks on any unit within 1 square, with full counter. Push (shield infantry): shoves an adjacent enemy back, no counter, full damage if blocked. Volley (archers): shoots an enemy exactly 2 squares away with no counter, plus 60% splash on one enemy beside it. Fire (Int 75+): burns an enemy within 3 squares. Each recovers in 3 turns.
