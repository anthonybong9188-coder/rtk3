    Generals

    Each general charges into battle with his troops. Generals with War above 80 add 1% damage per point, so Guan Yu (97) hits 17% harder.

    Victory

    Break the enemy commander's force (gold border) or every enemy unit. The attacker has 15 turns; after that the defender wins. You lose if your own commander's force breaks. Tap an enemy to attack it; your unit moves into range on its own.

    Specials (Shu only)

    Charge (cavalry): ×1.5 damage, weak counter, rides through to the square behind. Brace (spearmen): until Wei's turn ends, intercepts cavalry and spear attacks on any unit within 2 squares (diagonals included), with full counter. Joint attack (合, any unit): when 2 or more of your units stand next to one enemy, all of them strike it together, including units that already moved. Only the general who leads it takes a counter-attack, and no unit uses up its turn. Once per turn. Push (shield infantry): shoves an adjacent enemy back, no counter, full damage if blocked. Volley (archers): shoots an enemy exactly 2 squares away with no counter, plus 60% splash on one enemy beside it. Fire (Int 75+): only on an enemy standing in a forest, within 3 squares. Chance = (your Int − his Int) × 5%, so Int 90 against Int 75 is 75%. If it catches, it burns 20% of his troops (at least 500). Ambush 埋伏 (any unit, in a forest): hide and wait. The first enemy that moves next to you on its turn is ambushed, and his march stops there either way. If the ambush works (same chance and damage as Fire) he is also dazed and loses his turn. Either way you are revealed. Best in a forest beside a road the enemy will use. Rock Slide 落石 (Int 75+): same chance and damage, on an enemy standing beside a hill, within 3 squares. An enemy in a forest beside a hill can be hit by either. Each recovers in 3 turns.
